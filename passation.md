# Passation — Otman Kettani advisory website v2

## Positioning

Otman helps leaders find and deliver AI, automation and process opportunities that are worth doing. The honest answer may be AI, conventional automation, process change or no build. The site stays broad while offering narrow ways to begin.

Target: founders, COOs, customer/operations leaders and transformation leads at roughly 50–500 person companies. Geographic paths: Benelux, France, Morocco and MENA. Languages: English and French.

## Design direction

- Operator-led, editorial and contemporary — not a SaaS product page or an “AI agency” template.
- Strict sans-serif typography, near-black / cool off-white palette, one cobalt accent.
- Large type, asymmetric 1:3 grids, deliberate whitespace and rules instead of card grids.
- No stock imagery, gradients, glass effects, serif “consultancy” styling, decorative AI motifs, icon packs or excessive pills.
- Keep the section count low and the copy tight.
