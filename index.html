01 / The work

Measure the cost. Then choose the fix.

Most teams do not need another AI roadmap. They need someone to get close to the operation, price the manual work as it really runs, and make a hard call on what, if anything, deserves to be built.

01

Measure the baseline

Time, cost and error rate in the process as it runs today: real tickets, invoices, calls and handoffs, not the process diagram.

02

Choose the simplest fix

AI competes with plain automation, a workflow change and doing nothing. The least complex answer that beats the baseline wins.

03

Ship one scoped pilot

Agree the measure first, then pilot against the baseline. Scale, change or stop based on what the numbers show.

05 / Fit

Useful when the stakes are real and the answer is not obvious.

You own an operation, a customer experience or a transformation agenda. Manual work is compounding, a pilot is stuck, or vendors are selling answers before the problem is clear.

What size of company is the best fit?

Usually 50 to 500 people, without a large internal AI team. Smaller can work when the problem is specific and the relevant data exists.

Do you advise or build?

Both. I can advise the team, work alongside it, or hand over a documented pilot for your engineers to run.

What if the answer is “do not build”?

You get the decision, evidence and reasoning in writing. Stopping a weak project early is a useful outcome.
